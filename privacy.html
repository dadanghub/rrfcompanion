RRF Village Companion: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

RRF Village Companion (“the Site”) is an unofficial community guide. This policy explains what data it handles.

What we collect

We don’t ask for your name, email, wallet address or any account details, and we don’t run user accounts.

Data stored on your device

The Site saves your checklist progress in your browser’s local storage so it is still there after a refresh. This data stays on your device. We can’t see it, and you can clear it with the Reset button or your browser settings.

Marketplace data

When the Market tab is live and shows marketplace activity, it reads public blockchain and marketplace information about the Genesis Land collection. This is not data about you. The Site doesn’t request your wallet or look you up.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts loads the Site’s typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when the page opens. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Hosting (for example Vercel) may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type to run and secure the service.
  • Sky Mavis / Ronin Market API will be called by our server to fetch public marketplace data. Your browser doesn’t send it any personal information.

Cookies and tracking

We don’t use advertising cookies or analytics at this time. If that changes, we will update this page.

Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

Because we don’t hold personal data about you, there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove saved checklist data, use the Reset button or clear your browser’s site data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
